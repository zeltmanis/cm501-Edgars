Websites I love and hate

I love: GOV.UK

  • Accessibility: well structured with no errors (WAVE 0 errors, one h1, skip link, alt text on every image).
  • Performance: fast and light (Lighthouse 100, home page HTML only 85 KB).
  • Responsive: works on any screen size (viewport set, no horizontal scroll at 200% zoom).

I hate: Daily Mail

  • Performance: heavy (home page HTML alone is 1.48 MB with 70 scripts).
  • Visual design: a lot of visuals (476 images on the home page).
  • Security and privacy: a lot of ads that track my information (20 third-party ad and tracking domains, tracking cookie set before I agree).
